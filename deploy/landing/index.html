GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers

Is it free?

Yes. No account and no subscription

Does it send what I type anywhere?

No. The engine runs on your device, with no AI calls and no analytics

Does it work on a Mac or a phone?

Yes, in the browser. The download is for Windows only

Where is my history saved?

On the website, in the browser you use. In the Windows app, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SuperPrompt on your PC
